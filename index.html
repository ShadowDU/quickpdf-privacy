QuickPDF 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日

QuickPDF（套件名稱 com.shadowdu.quickpdf）是一款離線的 PDF 與 Word（.docx）閱讀器。本政策說明 App 如何處理你的資訊。簡單來說：QuickPDF 不收集、不傳送、也不分享任何個人資料。

我們不收集的資料

  • App 沒有網路權限，無法將任何資料傳送到網際網路。
  • 沒有帳號、登入、廣告、分析或當機回報服務。
  • 不會複製、上傳或修改你的檔案。

只存在你手機上的資料

為了顯示「最近開啟」清單並記住閱讀進度，App 會在你的裝置上（App 專屬的私人儲存空間）保存以下資訊：

  • 檔案名稱與檔案位置（Android 內容 URI）
  • 上次閱讀的頁數與總頁數
  • 上次開啟的時間

最多保存 20 筆。這些資料不會離開你的裝置。你可以在清單中長按項目將其移除（不會刪除檔案本身），或解除安裝 App 以清除所有資料。

檔案存取

QuickPDF 只會讀取你主動選擇的檔案：透過系統檔案選擇器，或從其他 App 選擇「以 QuickPDF 開啟」。App 不會要求讀取整個儲存空間的權限。為了讓你下次能從最近清單直接開啟，App 會保留對已選取檔案的唯讀存取權；從清單移除後即會釋放。

外部連結

若文件中含有網址連結且你點選它，連結會在你裝置上的瀏覽器中開啟，並適用該瀏覽器與網站的隱私權政策。

兒童

QuickPDF 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會收集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新此頁面與上方的生效日期。

聯絡我們

如有任何問題，請寄信至 info@shadowdu.com。

QuickPDF Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

QuickPDF (package name com.shadowdu.quickpdf) is an offline PDF and Word (.docx) reader. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: QuickPDF does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What we don't collect

  • The app has no internet permission and cannot send any data over the internet.
  • There are no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Your files are never copied, uploaded or modified.

Data kept only on your device

To show the recent-files list and remember your reading position, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • File name and location (an Android content URI)
  • Last page read and total page count
  • When the file was last opened

Up to 20 entries are kept. This data never leaves your device. You can remove an entry by long-pressing it in the list (the file itself is not deleted), or uninstall the app to erase everything.

File access

QuickPDF only reads files you choose: through the system file picker, or when you pick "Open with QuickPDF" in another app. It does not request broad storage permissions. So that recent files can be reopened later, the app keeps read-only access to files you have opened; that access is released when you remove the entry.

External links

If a document contains a web link and you tap it, the link opens in your device's browser, and that browser's and website's privacy policies apply.

Children

QuickPDF is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
